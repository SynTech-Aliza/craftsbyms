Behind the Crafts

Welcome to Crafts by Ms! What started as a passion for creating unique, detailed handmade pieces has grown into a space dedicated to crafting special keepsakes for your favorite moments.

Every item — from crocheted bouquets to customized gifts — is carefully crafted by hand with patience, care, and attention to detail. No two pieces are ever quite the same, because no two stories are either.

How to Order

Ordering your custom handcrafted piece is simple! All custom orders are processed directly through our official Facebook page.

1

Visit Our Facebook Page

Click the link below or head directly to Crafts by Ms on Facebook to see our full gallery and available crafts.

2

Send Us a Direct Message

Send us a message with your order details, including:

  • The item or design you'd like
  • Preferred colors, names, or personalization details
  • Your required event or delivery date
  • Any reference photos or inspiration images
3

Confirm & We Start Crafting

We'll reply to confirm your design details, price, and delivery timeline. Once confirmed, we'll craft your custom piece with love and package it carefully for delivery!
